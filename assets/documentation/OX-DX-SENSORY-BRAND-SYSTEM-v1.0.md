## Sensory Philosophy

Conceptual model:

**NOISE → SIGNAL → STRUCTURE → RESOLUTION**

This is not a literal pipeline.

Noise is not automatically wrong.

Signal is not automatically true.

Observation is not ownership.

Representation is not meaning.

Resolution is not reduction.

Unknown may remain unknown.

Silence may be the correct response.

The sensory system should make those boundaries felt without explaining them every time.

## Visual SFX

Canonical families:
- TRACE
- SCAN
- REVEAL
- RESOLVE
- FRAGMENT
- ALIGN
- CONNECT
- DISCONNECT
- SIGNAL
- DISTORT
- COMPRESS
- EXPAND
- FOCUS
- OBSERVE
- VERIFY
- UNKNOWN
- ERROR
- TRANSITION

These are visual-effect families only.

They are not ontology levels or engine phases.

### TRACE
Partial persistence and variable density reveal a relation or provenance path.

No glowing trail.

### SCAN
A bounded field changes density/witness state in a nonuniform sequence.

No horizontal movie-laser scanner.

### RESOLVE
Fragmented carriers become more coherent around a stable invariant while unsupported gaps stay open.

Resolution never means unknown became truth.

### OBSERVE
Witness frame, trace, and context appear around a target without visually absorbing or recoloring the target.

### UNKNOWN
Incomplete geometry, deliberate void, reduced persistence, and often silence.

Unknown is not error.

### VERIFY
Local irregularity settles into a stable supported state with a small confirmation cue.

No confetti or giant green flash.

### ERROR
Local disruption, break, offset, or signal loss.

No screen-wide panic animation.

`assets/sensory/visual/`

## Atmosphere

Desired feeling:

**contained · curious · focused**

Use:
- controlled density
- deep contrast
- localized light
- deliberate visual distance
- meaningful silence
- deterministic texture

Light is evidence.

Bright light should indicate something.

Avoid constant neon glow.

## Distortion

Distortion may communicate:
- uncertainty
- signal interference
- representation loss
- boundary crossing

It is never generic cyberpunk glitch.

Routine distortion is local and low intensity.

A distortion should settle or resolve into a readable state.

Recovery must not imply uncertainty became truth.

## Texture ↔ Sound

Cross-sensory relationships are conceptual, not literal one-to-one mappings.

Examples:
- noise texture ↔ soft filtered noise
- sharp fragment ↔ dry transient
- relation rail ↔ restrained resonance
- alignment ↔ reduced beating/instability
- resolution ↔ reduced noise + stable short resonance
- void ↔ silence

Do not sonify every visual mark.
