Master brand governance:

`brand-bible/governance.md`

Brand Bible:

`OX-DX-BRAND-BIBLE-v1.0.md`

## Asset classes

### MASTER
Authoritative editable geometry/source for identity-critical assets.

### DERIVED
PNG, WEBP, crop, format, or background export produced from a master/approved composition.

### VARIANT
Approved alternate treatment preserving canonical geometry.

### EXPERIMENTAL
Review-only candidate that cannot replace default identity without approval.

### DEPRECATED
Retained for migration/history but not recommended for new work.

## Identity-critical master assets

- `brand/logo/ox-dx-logo-primary.svg`
- `brand/icon/ox-dx-icon.svg`
- `brand/wordmark/ox-dx-wordmark.svg`
- `source/design/ox-dx-master-geometry.svg`

Master SVG geometry is canonical.

Raster exports are derived.

## Canonical supporting visual system

New supporting work uses:
- `brand/symbols/original/`
- `brand/glyphs/original/`
- `vector/original/`
- `web/*/original/`
- `documentation/visual/`
- `documentation/technical/design-tokens.md`

## Legacy supporting assets

Pre-Interrupted-Evidence-Field supporting symbols, glyphs, patterns, textures, backgrounds, hero/readme/release/social compositions remain in the repository when useful for history or migration.

They are **DEPRECATED FOR NEW WORK** unless the asset is an identity-preserving logo/icon/wordmark/lockup derivative.

Do not delete legacy assets merely to make the tree look cleaner.

## Rules

1. Master SVG is canonical.
2. Color/background variants preserve geometry.
3. New supporting work inherits Brand Bible + documented visual tokens.
4. **CLEAN STRUCTURE. DIRTY SURFACE.** Never corrupt master geometry.
5. Graphic constructs do not create ontology semantics.
6. EXPERIMENTAL assets require approval before promotion.
7. Do not commit proprietary fonts, generation caches, temporary previews, or fake model metadata.
8. Canonical/approved assets must be indexed.
9. Accessibility is part of asset validity.
10. Validation must pass before promotion.
