# OX-DX Conceptual Boundaries

These distinctions are part of the intellectual identity of OX-DX. Use them consistently in README, issues, PRs, releases, docs, CLI copy, and future UI.

## OBSERVATION ≠ OWNERSHIP

Something happening at a target does not make the event a structural child of that target.

**Not ownership. Observation.**

Use `OBSERVED_AT` when the relation is observational.

## PROVENANCE ≠ IDENTITY

A span or path tells us where evidence was seen. It does not, by itself, define the stable semantic identity of the resource.

**Location is a trace. Identity is a contract.**

## TYPE ≠ KIND

`TYPE` is one of the 49 canonical ontology levels. `KIND` is specialization within a type.

**Not another level. A specialization.**

## ONTOLOGY ≠ HIERARCHY

The ontology gives canonical type order. Structural containment is only one edge class in the graph.

**Not one giant tree. A resolution spine over a graph.**

## RESOLUTION ≠ REDUCTION

Moving toward finer detail must not erase distinctions, provenance, or uncertainty merely to make the model smaller.

**Resolution, not reduction.**

## PROJECTION ≠ REWRITING

Projection creates an explicit justified view at another ontology level. It does not mutate the native source into that view.

**Not replacement. Projection.**

## EVIDENCE ≠ TRUTH

Evidence supports a claim. It does not grant unlimited authority to infer beyond what was observed.

**Not absolute truth. Evidence.**

## GRAPH ≠ TREE

A tree encodes parent/child structure. A graph can express reference, dependency, causation, projection, representation, and observation without pretending those relations are ownership.

**The graph connects.**

## NATIVE STRUCTURE ≠ CANONICAL STRUCTURE

Native systems keep their own language/framework structure. Canonical ontology types provide interoperability vocabulary around that evidence.

**Native structure stays native.**

## REPRESENTATION ≠ MEANING

Bytes, tokens, characters, and encoded data are representations. Interpretation requires context; encoding does not automatically establish semantic identity.

**Concrete form is not the whole meaning.**

## IMPLEMENTED ≠ PLANNED

The presence of a type, edge name, roadmap phase, or brand statement does not prove the corresponding engine capability is complete.

**Say what exists. Mark what remains.**
