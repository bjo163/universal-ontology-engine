# FROM STRUCTURE TO EXPERIENCE.

**FROM STRUCTURE TO EXPERIENCE.** is the primary OX-DX brand statement.

It describes direction, not a claim that every part of that direction is implemented today.

## Structure

Structure is where observable organization becomes describable: repositories, sources, implementation containers, modules, components, elements, and the relations that organize or connect them.

The current Rust engine has strong evidence here through canonical registry validation, graph invariants, read-only discovery, Rust AST observation, and deterministic cross-language syntax observation.

## Experience

Experience is the broader endpoint: how structured evidence, semantic views, runtime behavior, representation, resolution, and later query/certification can become interpretable and useful to people or systems.

Experience is not currently one of the 49 canonical ontology levels.

Experience is not currently a fully implemented subsystem.

## The communication boundary

Say:

> OX-DX moves from structure toward increasingly interpretable evidence and representation.

Do not say:

> OX-DX already implements the complete experience layer.

The tagline remains valid because it expresses the project trajectory while status language tells the reader what exists today.

**Brand direction and engineering status must remain separate.**
