# FROM UNIVERSE TO BIT.

**FROM UNIVERSE TO BIT.** is the canonical OX-DX scale statement.

It is a narrative expression of the Universal Ontology's resolution span: from broad managed concepts such as `UNIVERSE` toward increasingly fine semantic, dynamic, and representational types, ending at `BIT`.

## What it means

- the ontology has a stable ordered vocabulary across very different scales;
- the 49 types form a resolution spine over a graph;
- a resource may be described or projected at multiple relevant scales;
- intermediate levels may remain unmaterialized when evidence does not justify them;
- finer representation does not erase higher-level identity or meaning.

## What it does not mean

It does **not** mean OX-DX explains the physical universe.

It does **not** mean every object physically passes through 49 nested boxes.

It does **not** mean the ontology is a universal filesystem, AST, compiler IR, or binary layout.

It does **not** mean BIT is more authoritative than the evidence available at another level.

## Usage

Use as a signature line for ontology scale, resolution-spine explanation, architecture storytelling, documentation footers, and selected release/social communication.

Do not use it as proof of an implementation capability that remains planned.

**Scale → resolution.**

Not:

**hierarchy → ownership.**
