# OX-DX Resolution Language

## Canonical definition

**Resolution means moving across increasingly specific conceptual or representational scales while preserving relevant distinctions, provenance, and explicit relations.**

The word describes a change in descriptive scale. It does not imply that lower levels are more “real,” that every resource must traverse every level, or that fine-grained representation replaces higher-level meaning.

## Resolution is

- evidence-driven;
- bounded by what can be observed or justified;
- compatible with skipped/unmaterialized canonical levels;
- explicit about projection and representation;
- careful about identity and provenance;
- able to move toward finer detail without converting every relation into containment.

## Resolution is not

- destruction;
- simplification by deletion;
- forced hierarchy;
- loss of provenance;
- a mandatory 49-step traversal;
- a claim that BIT is “truer” than higher-level concepts;
- permission to infer semantics from filenames or paths alone.

## Preferred language

Use:

- “resolve toward a finer representation”
- “resolve evidence through the canonical vocabulary”
- “increase resolution while preserving provenance”
- “the resolution spine provides ordered vocabulary”
- “intermediate levels may remain unmaterialized”
- “projection is explicit”

Avoid:

- “drill down through 49 nested containers”
- “reduce everything to bits”
- “the lower level reveals ultimate truth”
- “force the object into the ontology”
- “flatten the source into one hierarchy”

## Communication test

Before using **resolve**, ask:

1. What evidence is moving or being described?
2. What distinction is preserved?
3. Is the relation containment, projection, observation, or representation?
4. What boundary prevents overclaiming?

If those cannot be answered, use a more precise verb.
