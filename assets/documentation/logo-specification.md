The approved **OX-DX master mark geometry** is authoritative.

The supporting visual system is the **Interrupted Evidence Field**. It grows around the mark; it does not redraw the mark.

## Geometry

The approved symbol uses:
- opposed hard-edged/chamfered frame geometry;
- a centered circular observation field specific to the master mark;
- a rotated square/diamond resolved signal;
- square caps/hard corners;
- zero decorative radius.

The circular field is logo-specific. Supporting visual nodes are not required to be circles.

## Clear space

Keep at least **1/4 of the symbol width** clear on every side.

## Minimum size

- 16–24 px: simplified favicon/icon form.
- 32 px and above: standard icon geometry.
- Use symbol-only before wordmark counters/chamfers become unreadable.

## Safe backgrounds

Preferred:
- White geometry on Void / Charcoal / Graphite.
- Black geometry on White.

Cyan may reinforce the observation node but is not required for recognition.

Do not place the master mark directly on aggressive texture. Give it a quiet field.

## Monochrome

The mark must remain complete in pure black or white.

Never encode logo recognition only through accent color.

## Wordmark

The wordmark is custom vector geometry.

Do not retype, stretch, skew, rotate, outline, glow, texture, or substitute another display font.

## Supporting symbols

Supporting symbols/glyphs may inherit hard edges, tension, diamond witness logic, and zero-radius construction.

They remain visual notation, not ontology semantics.

## Asset classes

- **MASTER** — authoritative identity geometry/source.
- **DERIVED** — format/crop/raster export from canonical geometry.
- **VARIANT** — approved monochrome/background treatment preserving geometry.
- **DEPRECATED** — retained legacy supporting material, not for new work.

Never recreate a master asset from a screenshot.
