# OX-DX Certainty Language

OX-DX interfaces must avoid fake certainty.

## KNOWN

Use only when a value or relation is established by current product context and underlying source.

Do not use known as a synonym for absolute truth.

## OBSERVED

Directly recorded from source or input.

Observation says what was seen, not everything that can be concluded.

## DERIVED

Produced through a defined transformation from evidence.

Derived is not direct observation.

## INFERRED

Interpretive conclusion based on evidence or rules.

Only use this distinction if the product or engine genuinely exposes inference as a meaningful category.

## PROJECTED

An explicit alternate or canonical view linked to source evidence.

Projection is not rewriting.

## UNKNOWN

Current evidence cannot establish the requested value or relation.

Unknown is a valid result.

## UNVERIFIED

A result or claim exists but validation at the relevant scope is not established.

## No fake confidence score

Do not display numerical confidence, percentage certainty, or qualitative confidence levels unless the underlying engine defines, computes, and documents that measure.

## Copy patterns

Preferred:
> Observed from Cargo.toml.

Preferred:
> Projection is supported by this relation.

Preferred:
> Unknown: no supported evidence observed.

Avoid:
> Probably correct.

Avoid:
> The system is confident.

## Visual rule

Certainty distinctions combine text with witness/break geometry, relation/persistence, and optionally restrained color.

Opacity alone is not a certainty scale.
