# OX-DX Intelligence / AI Boundary

If future AI capabilities are introduced:

**AI MAY ASSIST INTERPRETATION.**

**AI MUST NOT REWRITE EVIDENCE.**

This document does not assert that an AI product capability currently exists.

## Separation

AI-generated suggestions must remain visually and semantically distinct from:
- observed evidence;
- canonical ontology;
- verified facts;
- engine output;
- user-authored source.

## Suggestion anatomy

A future AI suggestion should state:
- that it is generated/assisted;
- source evidence it used when available;
- what is interpretation versus observation;
- uncertainty/boundary;
- action required to accept/apply anything.

## Never

Do not:
- silently convert AI output into canonical classification;
- label generated interpretation as OBSERVED;
- overwrite provenance;
- invent evidence;
- use AI confidence as technical verification;
- make a glowing brain/chat orb the product identity.

## Product identity

OX-DX identity remains:
- evidence;
- ontology;
- graph;
- resolution;
- provenance;
- inspection.

AI, if added, is one capability inside the instrument.

It is not the instrument itself.

## Mutation

Any future AI-assisted mutation requires explicit review/commit semantics and is outside this handoff.
