# OX-DX Product Interaction Principles

Canonical product verbs:

**INSPECT · TRACE · OBSERVE · CONNECT · RESOLVE · VERIFY · EXPLORE**

Use generic verbs such as Open, Manage, Configure, or Submit only where they are actually the clearest conventional action.

## INSPECT

Reveal evidence, relations, status, and context around a target.

## TRACE

Follow relation or provenance without pretending it is containment.

## OBSERVE

Show observed state/evidence with explicit source and target relationship.

## CONNECT

Inspect or establish a relationship only when the product supports that operation.

## RESOLVE

Move toward finer justified context or representation.

## VERIFY

Run or inspect a named validation process.

Do not use VERIFY for subjective approval.

## EXPLORE

Traverse graph/context without implying mutation.

## Interaction states

### Hover → preview
Reveal lightweight context only.

### Focus → inspect-ready
Establish accessible keyboard witness.

### Select → resolve focus
Commit current inspection target.

### Expand → increase visible detail
More evidence, context, or representation.

### Trace → follow relationship
Preserve source and destination context.

## Safety

Before mutation or interpretation:
- show context;
- show target;
- show evidence where relevant;
- explain effect.

## Motion

Interaction motion must express relation, witness, or resolution. Decorative animation is not a product verb.
