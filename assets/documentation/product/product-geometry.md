# OX-DX Product Geometry

Product geometry derives from the Interrupted Evidence Field.

## Container geometry

Default containers are inspection fields, not soft floating cards.

Prefer:
- open or interrupted boundaries;
- square/miter corners;
- intentional voids;
- partial frames.

Closed containers are valid when literal containment or usability requires them.

## Panel geometry

Inspection panels use:
- clear structural edge;
- one meaningful interruption or witness cue where appropriate;
- low/no decorative radius;
- limited shadow.

## Node geometry

Default OX-DX-native node:
- cut-square or diamond witness;
- optional open-frame context;
- explicit label.

Use simpler rectangles when dense technical comprehension benefits.

## Edge geometry

Relations use Persistence Rails.

Direction can come from persistence, endpoint behavior, label placement, or arrowhead where conventional semantics help.

## Indicator geometry

Use:
- diamond witness;
- split anchor;
- break;
- source notch;
- boundary cut.

Do not invent a new symbol for every state.

## Selection geometry

Selected:
- add witness frame/anchor;
- preserve object geometry;
- avoid total recoloring.

## Focus geometry

Focus:
- keyboard-visible open witness outline;
- high-contrast;
- distinct from selection.

## Construction units

Use canonical tokens:
- 3 px micro offset;
- 7 px base;
- 14 px anchor/relation break;
- 21 px observation offset;
- 49 px macro field.

## Radius

Canonical construction radius is 0 for OX-DX-native geometry.

Platform controls may retain conventional radius if accessibility/usability demands it. Do not fake originality by making every native control angular.
