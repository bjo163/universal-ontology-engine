# OX-DX Product Motion

Canonical visual source: `../visual/motion-grammar.md`.

## Axiom

**MOTION IS RESOLUTION.**

## Operational rule

**RESOLVE, DON'T DECORATE.**

## Product mappings

### Object enters context
Relations may reveal while the object anchor remains stable.

### Selection
Evidence may expand around the selected target rather than scaling the target dramatically.

### Resolution change
Finer detail emerges while at least one context invariant remains visible.

### Observation
A witness trace/marker appears without moving the observed object into an observation container.

### Provenance
A source tail may reveal from object toward origin.

## Timing tokens

Use existing canonical timings:
- 98 ms micro;
- 196 ms witness;
- 294 ms resolve;
- 490 ms field.

No product-specific timing scale is introduced here.

## Avoid

- animation with no semantic purpose;
- ambient parallax;
- perpetual glitch;
- particles;
- bounce as default feedback;
- fake “scanning” effects;
- opacity-only resolution when geometry should change.

## Reduced motion

With reduced motion:
- render resolved state directly where practical;
- preserve spatial/semantic distinction;
- do not hide information;
- avoid requiring animation to understand relation or status.
