# OX-DX Resolution UI

The 49-level Universal Ontology may become a product interaction primitive.

It must never become a fake 49-folder tree.

## Interaction model

```text
HIGH CONTEXT
    ↓
CURRENT LEVEL
    ↓
RELATED EVIDENCE
    ↓
DEEPER RESOLUTION
```

The user should feel like they are resolving structure, not browsing a mandatory nested directory.

## Resolution Spine

The product may show:
- current canonical TYPE;
- nearby broader or finer TYPEs;
- materialized nodes and evidence;
- gaps where intermediate levels are unmaterialized;
- related projections and representations.

## Gaps are valid

If native evidence skips an intermediate canonical level, preserve the gap. Do not invent placeholder nodes merely to make the spine visually complete.

## Resolution action

A resolution action should answer:
- what finer evidence becomes available?
- what context remains stable?
- what relation connects the views?
- what is representation versus semantic identity?

## Preserve context

At deeper resolution retain current scope, a path back to broader context, and identity/provenance boundaries.

## Responsive behavior

On compact screens show the current level, immediate broader/finer affordance, and one context indicator. Do not squeeze all 49 labels into a mobile rail.

## Technical boundary

The product may only materialize levels supported by repository or engine data. A visually possible level must remain distinct from an observed/materialized node.
