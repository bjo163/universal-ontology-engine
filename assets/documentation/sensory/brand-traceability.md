# OX-DX Brand → Sensory Traceability

| Brand principle | Sensory principle | Visual effect | Sonic effect | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EVIDENCE FIRST.** | Effects reveal state/evidence | TRACE / REVEAL | restrained source transient | evidence carrier persists then fades |
| **DON'T FORCE THE SHAPE.** | Effect follows real context | context-aware SCAN | silence unless inspection matters | bounded field response |
| **OBSERVATION IS NOT OWNERSHIP.** | Observation is subtle | OBSERVE witness around unchanged target | quiet Witness tone | non-invasive bracket reveal |
| **PROVENANCE IS NOT IDENTITY.** | Source is secondary | provenance TRACE | faint Grain/Breath | tail reveals toward source |
| **THE GRAPH CONNECTS.** | Relation has persistence | CONNECT | paired Rail | endpoints remain distinct while relation appears |
| **RESOLUTION, NOT REDUCTION.** | Resolution increases coherence without false truth | RESOLVE | Settle | fragmented carriers clarify around invariant |
| **NO FALSE CERTAINTY.** | Unknown may remain quiet | UNKNOWN | silence | no forced completion |
| **MOTION IS RESOLUTION.** | Motion explains change | RESOLVE / ALIGN | Settle | persistence/density changes with stable anchor |
| **CLEAN STRUCTURE. DIRTY SURFACE.** | Friction is local and deterministic | FRAGMENT / DISTORT | soft interrupted noise | bounded dropout then recovery |
| **ORDER INSIDE CHAOS.** | Noise may organize without disappearing completely | SIGNAL / ALIGN | Grain → Rail | local cadence becomes readable |
| **INTERACTION = EXAMINING EVIDENCE.** | Routine feedback is restrained | FOCUS / TRACE | usually silent or Witness | local reveal only |
| **VERIFICATION IS SCOPED.** | Verification settles | VERIFY | short Settle | local stabilization + label |
| **UNKNOWN IS VALID.** | Silence can communicate restraint | UNKNOWN | none | still state / open boundary |
| **Gen-Z clarity.** | Fast, direct sensory response | compact witness cue | short dry transient | 98–196 ms routine |
| **Intelligent precision.** | Every cue has a reason | exact local geometry | related sonic family | canonical timing/fallback |
| **Quranic depth, not authority.** | measure/order/quiet can inspire rhythm | measured field behavior | restrained pauses/resonance | no sacred icon/text/sound symbolism |

## Traceability rule

A new sensory effect must have a chain:

```text
BRAND PRINCIPLE
      ↓
SENSORY PRINCIPLE
      ↓
EVENT
      ↓
VISUAL / AUDIO / MOTION
      ↓
ACCESSIBLE FALLBACK
```

If the chain cannot be explained, the effect is decorative and should not become canonical.
