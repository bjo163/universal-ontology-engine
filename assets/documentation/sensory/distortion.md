# OX-DX Distortion Language

Distortion communicates a condition.

It is not a generic cyberpunk glitch.

## Valid meanings

Distortion may represent:
- uncertainty;
- signal interference;
- representation loss;
- boundary crossing;
- interrupted continuity.

## Intensity

### Level 1 — MICRO
One local offset/dropout.

### Level 2 — SUBTLE
Several related segments lose/shift persistence.

### Level 3 — NOTICEABLE
A bounded field is visibly disrupted but remains readable.

### Level 4 — MAJOR
Reserved for major product/brand transitions.

### Level 5 — CINEMATIC
Brand film/installations only.

Routine UI should rarely exceed Level 2.

## Duration

Use canonical duration families:
- 98 ms;
- 196 ms;
- 294 ms;
- 490 ms.

Distortion should resolve or settle.

Do not leave continuous animated corruption running in the background.

## Scope

Distortion must be local:
- relation;
- representation surface;
- bounded region;
- transition layer.

Never distort the whole screen for a local error.

## Recovery

Recovery communicates:
- restored readability;
- settled state;
- persistent unresolved gaps if evidence is still missing.

Recovery must not imply that uncertainty became truth.

## Audio relation

Possible pairing:
- short soft noise interruption;
- broken transient;
- slight unstable resonance.

Avoid bit-crusher clichés unless a representation/encoding context actually justifies that sound.
