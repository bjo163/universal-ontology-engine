# OX-DX Sensory Grammar

The sensory system coordinates:

**VISUAL · AUDIO · MOTION · SPACE · TEXT · SILENCE**

No channel is allowed to contradict another.

## Event specification contract

Every sensory event must define:

### TRIGGER
What actually happened?

### VISUAL BEHAVIOR
What geometry, persistence, trace, density, or surface change occurs?

### SONIC BEHAVIOR
What sound occurs, if any?

### MOTION BEHAVIOR
What transforms over time?

### TEXT BEHAVIOR
What label/message communicates the state?

### INTENSITY
Use the smallest level that communicates the event.

### DURATION
Prefer canonical timing families.

### ACCESSIBILITY
How is meaning available without color, audio, or motion?

### REDUCED-MOTION / SILENT MODE
What remains when motion/audio is removed?

## Cross-sensory primitives

### PERSISTENCE
Visual: continuity of rail/geometry.  
Audio: sustained resonance or decay.  
Meaning: relation or signal remains present.

### BREAK
Visual: measured gap/interruption.  
Audio: interrupted transient/noise.  
Meaning: continuity is not established.

### WITNESS
Visual: offset bracket/anchor.  
Audio: restrained inspection transient.  
Meaning: something was observed/focused.

### TRACE
Visual: secondary rail/tail.  
Audio: faint textured continuation.  
Meaning: source or relation can be followed.

### SETTLE
Visual: local geometry stabilizes.  
Audio: unstable partial/noise reduces into a stable short resonance.  
Meaning: a verification or resolution event reached a supported state.

### VOID
Visual: intentional empty space.  
Audio: silence.  
Meaning: unknown, pause, boundary, or non-event.

## Conceptual sensory flow

`NOISE → SIGNAL → STRUCTURE → RESOLUTION`

Use only when appropriate.

Do not imply:
- noise = wrong;
- signal = true;
- resolution = certainty;
- silence = failure.

## Duration family

Reuse canonical timing:
- 98 ms — micro;
- 196 ms — witness;
- 294 ms — resolve/local;
- 490 ms — field/major.

Brand-film/major sensory moments may combine these units rather than invent arbitrary timing systems.
