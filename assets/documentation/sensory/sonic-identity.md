# OX-DX Sonic Identity

The sonic world should express:

**structure · signal · resolution · observation · depth · precision**

Character:

- minimal;
- textural;
- precise;
- slightly organic;
- slightly mechanical;
- deep;
- quietly unusual.

## Sonic DNA

OX-DX sound is built from five related materials:

### GRAIN
Very short dry textural transient.

Use for focus, selection, source contact, micro-events.

### BREATH / NOISE
Soft filtered noise with short controlled envelope.

Use for trace, interference, reveal, unknown boundaries.

Never use loud white-noise sweeps.

### RAIL
Short low-mid resonance or restrained harmonic tone.

Use for relation, connect, settle.

### WITNESS
A precise click/transient with a faint resonant tail.

Use for inspect/observe/focus.

### SETTLE
A brief unstable-to-stable texture where noise/inharmonicity reduces and a stable resonance remains.

Use for resolve/verify.

## Frequency character

Favor:
- low-mid body;
- controlled upper detail;
- no piercing high-frequency “digital beep” identity;
- no sub-bass trailer boom.

## Tonality

Do not make every sound tonal.

When pitch is used:
- prefer sparse partials/dyads;
- avoid major/minor corporate melodies;
- avoid “success chime” tropes;
- let stability come from reduced beating/noise rather than a cheerful cadence.

## Spatial behavior

Routine UI sounds remain mostly centered/mono-compatible.

Spatial movement may support relation tracing in immersive contexts, but spatialization never carries unique meaning.

## Dynamic behavior

Quiet by default.

Criticality increases clarity/weight before loudness.

## Relationship families

- **INSPECT / OBSERVE / FOCUS** share Witness DNA.
- **TRACE / CONNECT / DISCOVER** share Grain + Noise + Rail DNA.
- **RESOLVE / VERIFY / SUCCESS** share Settle DNA.
- **WARNING / ERROR** interrupt the same material rather than entering a different cinematic sound universe.
