# OX-DX Texture ↔ Sound Relationship

Visual texture and sound share behavior, not literal synchronization.

## NOISE TEXTURE ↔ SOFT NOISE AUDIO

Use when evidence is incomplete, entering context, or experiencing interference.

Do not equate noise with incorrectness.

## SHARP FRAGMENT ↔ SHORT TRANSIENT

A bounded break can pair with a dry Grain/Witness transient.

No explosion.

## RELATION RAIL ↔ RESTRAINED RESONANCE

A visible relation can pair with a short Rail resonance.

The sound does not imply the relation is verified unless verification actually occurred.

## ALIGNMENT ↔ REDUCED INSTABILITY

As geometry aligns, beating/noise may reduce.

Do not resolve into a triumphant chord.

## RESOLUTION ↔ LOWER NOISE + STABLE SHORT RESONANCE

Clarity increases.

Unsupported gaps remain audible/visible as absence rather than being filled.

## VOID ↔ SILENCE

Silence is the strongest cross-sensory mapping for unknown, pause, or deliberate absence.

## Rule

Do not sonify every visual texture.

Use cross-sensory pairing only when:
- the event matters;
- sound improves awareness;
- repetition will not create fatigue;
- silent mode retains full meaning.
