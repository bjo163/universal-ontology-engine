# OX-DX Video Language

OX-DX video uses the same sensory grammar as product interaction, at greater duration/intensity only when the context justifies it.

## Opening

Canonical short opening concept:

`darkness → subtle evidence → trace → structure → OX-DX`

The logo should **emerge**, not explode.

Suggested 3-stage timing:
- evidence fragment;
- relation/trace alignment;
- mark reveal + short settle.

Do not begin with a giant logo hit.

## Logo reveal

Use:
- measured fragments;
- witness alignment;
- persistence becoming coherent;
- quiet sonic settle.

Never distort the master logo itself into random glitch.

Supporting geometry may carry disturbance around a stable logo.

## Title reveal

A title may enter through:
- field opening;
- line persistence;
- void clearing;
- local reveal from evidence axis.

Avoid generic blur/fade-only title treatment as the signature.

## System reveal

Architecture should appear as relations and contexts becoming observable, not boxes flying in.

## Architecture animation

Use:
- open context frames;
- relation rails;
- witness anchors;
- provenance tails.

Maintain readable labels.

## Ontology animation

Show resolution/spine behavior.

Do not render 49 nested boxes as spectacle.

## Resolution sequence

Use the canonical idea:

`fragmented evidence → clearer supported structure`

Keep unresolved gaps open.

## Transition families

Preferred:
- structure separates into layers;
- relationship becomes visible;
- fragment aligns;
- observation frame appears;
- signal converges;
- context density changes.

Avoid default:
- swipe;
- spin;
- flash;
- zoom tunnel;
- generic glitch transition.

## Closing

Let motion settle.

Prefer:
- stable mark;
- one canonical line;
- silence or sonic-logo tail.

Do not end with excessive trailer impact.

## Editing pace

Technical/explanatory video:
- deliberate;
- enough time to read labels;
- no constant cuts.

Brand film may use intensity level 4–5, but the grammar remains restrained and evidence-derived.
