# OX-DX Spoken Voice Identity

This defines future narration/spoken-system direction.

It does not require a synthetic voice and does not authorize an AI persona.

## Tone

- calm;
- intelligent;
- human;
- precise;
- slightly intimate;
- reflective without mysticism;
- confident without certainty theater.

## Pace

Moderate-slow.

Technical words get enough space to remain intelligible.

Pause before or after a major distinction instead of emphasizing it through dramatic volume.

## Clarity

Pronounce:
- file names;
- acronyms;
- technical relation names;
- numbers;
- versions;

clearly and literally.

Do not smooth technical language into vague marketing speech.

## Presence

Close and present, not announcer-like.

Avoid:
- corporate commercial voice;
- trailer narrator;
- robotic “AI assistant” delivery;
- whisper-ASMR styling;
- overacted solemnity.

## Silence

Pauses are part of the voice identity.

Use silence to separate:
- observation;
- distinction;
- consequence.

Do not fill every gap with ambient speech.

## Pronunciation

### OX-DX
Primary spoken form:

**“O-X, D-X”**

Spell both letter pairs clearly.

Do not default to “ox-dex” unless a future brand decision explicitly changes pronunciation.

### Universal Ontology & Experience Engine
Say:

**“Universal Ontology and Experience Engine.”**

Use “and” in speech for the ampersand.

### Universal Ontology
Say normally:

**“Universal Ontology.”**

## Voice copy

Prefer short declarative lines:

> Evidence observed.

> Relationship traced.

> Verification complete.

> No supported relation observed.

Avoid anthropomorphic certainty:

> I know what this is.

> I understand everything.

## Quranic boundary

Spoken philosophical material follows the same Brand Bible boundary:
- reflective inspiration is allowed;
- no fabricated verse;
- no invented Arabic;
- no claim of divine technical authority.
