# OX-DX Typography

Brand Bible authority:

`../OX-DX-BRAND-BIBLE-v1.0.md`

The OX-DX wordmark is custom vector geometry and is **not a font**. Distribution SVGs outline the lettering and require no installed font.

## DISPLAY / IDENTITY

- Family: canonical OX-DX vector wordmark only.
- Weight: defined by geometry.
- Usage: brand name and primary identity lockups.
- Rule: never retype `OX-DX` to replace the master wordmark in identity-critical placements.

## HEADLINE

- Family: `Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`.
- Weight: 650–800 where supported; otherwise 700.
- Typical size: 40–72 px desktop, 30–48 px compact layouts.
- Line-height: 0.98–1.08.
- Letter-spacing: `-0.03em` to `-0.01em`.
- Case: sentence case for explanatory headlines; uppercase for canonical short principles only.

## BODY

- Family: `Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`.
- Weight: 400–500.
- Typical size: 16–20 px.
- Line-height: 1.45–1.65.
- Letter-spacing: normal.
- Case: normal sentence case.

## MONO

- Family: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`.
- Weight: 400–550.
- Typical size: 13–16 px.
- Line-height: 1.4–1.55.
- Usage: paths, commands, ontology names, evidence, provenance, annotations.

## TECHNICAL LABEL

- Family: monospace stack.
- Weight: 550–650.
- Typical size: 10–12 px.
- Line-height: 1.2–1.35.
- Letter-spacing: `0.10em`–`0.16em`.
- Case: uppercase.

## Hierarchy

`WORDMARK → HEADLINE → DESCRIPTOR → BODY → TECHNICAL LABEL`

Typography should feel **editorial, technical, hard-edged, contemporary, and readable**.

Do not use rounded startup fonts, decorative long-form faces, or illegible cyberpunk typography.

Do not depend on proprietary font binaries.

The system must not call itself “brutalist” as a brand category. Hard edges are construction logic, not membership in a design trend.
