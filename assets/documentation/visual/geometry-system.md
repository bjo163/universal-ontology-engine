# OX-DX Geometry System

Supporting geometry derives from the approved master mark: opposed hard-edged frames, diagonal convergence, square/diamond witness geometry, non-rounded construction, and negative space. The master logo itself is not redrawn.

## Interrupted Dyad
The base construction is two non-identical open frames facing a shared field. One side differs in break position, depth, persistence, or anchor offset. This creates partial symmetry rather than ornamental symmetry.

## Rules
1. No default circles. Use square/diamond witness geometry unless a literal circular measurement is required.
2. Frames stay open. At least one interruption prevents a generic card reading.
3. Diagonals are functional. Use 45° cuts at convergence, split, or witness points only.
4. Continuity can change from solid stroke to segmented rail while preserving axis.
5. Nesting is unequal. Repeated interruption points must not form generic concentric patterns.
6. Resolution preserves an invariant: anchor coordinate, dominant axis, break alignment, or trace origin.
