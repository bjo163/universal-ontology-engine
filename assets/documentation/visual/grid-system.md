# OX-DX Grid System

The OX-DX grid is a **Seven-Lane Interrupted Field** rather than a generic 12-column system.

## Base
`u = 7 px` and `field = 49 px = 7u`.

## Seven lanes
Recommended default:
- 3 lanes = primary content field;
- 1 lane = intentional void/interruption;
- 3 lanes = observation/context field.

The empty lane may move; it should not always sit in the center.

## Three simultaneous grids
**Structural field** controls large composition and primary content.

**Observational field** is offset by 21 px and carries witness brackets, provenance, labels, or context.

**Micro-detail field** uses 7 px alignment for rails, grains, and annotations.

## Asymmetric anchors
Prefer 3/7, 4/7, or 5/7 positions over permanent center bias.

## Compact behavior
At small sizes preserve one structural axis and one witness offset. Collapse secondary lanes rather than shrinking every element.
