# OX-DX Iconography

OX-DX iconography derives from the approved mark and Interrupted Evidence Field.

## Artboards
49, 98, and 196 units/pixels depending on export.

## Stroke
- primary: 7 at 196 scale;
- secondary: 3;
- minimum critical stroke at small sizes: visually equivalent to ~2 px.

## Fill
Use fill for witness anchors, major active grains, or optical correction. Do not fill entire frames by default.

## Corner
Square/miter. A single 45° cut may indicate split, convergence, or witness geometry.

## Negative space
Every icon should preserve one meaningful void. Do not close every contour.

## Small-size behavior
At ≤24 px remove 1 px annotations, simplify paired rails, enlarge the witness anchor if necessary, and keep breaks visible.

## Library rule
Do not redraw every conventional icon in OX-DX styling. For universal actions like search, close, download, or accessibility, established icons may be more usable.
