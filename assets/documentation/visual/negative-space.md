# OX-DX Negative Space

Negative space is an active primitive.

## Void Slot
A **void slot** is a measured empty lane or gap that interrupts otherwise coherent structure.

Preferred widths:
- `u` for local uncertainty;
- `3u` for relational separation;
- `7u` for major context break.

These are design roles, not technical uncertainty metrics.

A void may communicate unknown, unobserved, distance, boundary, pause, resolution gap, or missing context. The surrounding annotation determines interpretation.

## Rules
1. Never fill every field.
2. One strong void is better than many arbitrary gaps.
3. Labels may sit beside a void, not inside it.
4. A relation may stop at a void instead of pretending continuity.
5. Surface texture must not contaminate the clearest major void.

OX-DX should be recognizable partly because **it refuses to fill space the evidence does not justify.**
