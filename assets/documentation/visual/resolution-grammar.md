# OX-DX Resolution Grammar

Resolution is a visual operation, not zoom.

```text
FIELD → RIFT → TRACE → PULSE → GRAIN
```

These are graphic states only, not an engine pipeline.

## FIELD
Structured context with frames, rails, anchors, and voids.

## RIFT
Measured continuity breaks while anchor and dominant axis remain.

## TRACE
Only persistent carriers remain; provenance echoes appear.

## PULSE
Continuous rails become deterministic cadence.

## GRAIN
Cadence resolves into discrete square/diamond units.

## Resolution invariant
At every transition at least one property remains stable:
- anchor;
- axis;
- break coordinate;
- origin trace.

Without an invariant, the motion is decoration.

## Reverse resolution
Grain may re-accumulate into pulse, trace, rift, and field, but missing fragments remain visibly missing. Reverse motion must not imply recovered evidence.

This grammar must never be presented as a replacement for the 49 canonical types, as runtime execution flow, or as proof that every object reaches BIT.
