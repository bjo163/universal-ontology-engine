# OX-DX Surface System

Dark, light, inverse, and high-contrast surfaces preserve the same geometry but do not blindly invert every detail.

## Dark
Void/Charcoal canvas; White primary geometry; Cyan observation; Muted trace at readable contrast.

## Light
White canvas; Black/Void geometry; Cyan may remain but must be paired with shape; Graphite trace. Reduce disturbance density because dark marks accumulate visually.

## Inverse
For compact brand placement. Prefer monochrome and remove optional textures/annotations before adding complexity.

## High contrast
Use pure black/white, primary 7 px strokes at symbol scale, no critical 1 px lines, no texture, and no color-only meaning.

Structural properties never invert:
- dropout positions;
- frame breaks;
- anchor coordinates;
- relation persistence.

Texture intensity and trace darkness may adapt.
