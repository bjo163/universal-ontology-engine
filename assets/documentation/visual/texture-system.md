# OX-DX Texture System

Canonical public principle:

**CLEAN STRUCTURE. DIRTY SURFACE.**

“Dirty” is a brand expression for controlled friction.

The implementation remains deterministic surface disturbance, never random dirt.

## Texture vocabulary

### Trace Erosion
A thin line loses measured segments as it moves away from an anchor.

### Signal Loss
Parallel rails drop out at different deterministic positions.

### Compression Scar
Short segments collapse toward one boundary and leave a hard gap.

### Scan Break
A narrow band offsets or deletes geometry across a field.

### Boundary Wear
A frame edge loses persistence near a break while construction remains precise.

## Rules

- never apply texture to the master logo;
- never damage critical text;
- do not use full-canvas paper grain by default;
- avoid fake VHS, random film scratches, dust, and arbitrary speckle;
- texture must be reproducible from fixed geometry;
- use no more than one primary disturbance class per composition.

## Intensity

- `0` — clean;
- `1` — trace-level disturbance;
- `2` — editorial disturbance;
- `3` — poster/release maximum.

Critical diagrams normally use 0–1.

## Precision × damage

The undamaged geometry remains mathematically precise.

Damage affects persistence, not construction logic.
