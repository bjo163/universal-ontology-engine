# OX-DX Typographic Grammar

Typography participates in observation and resolution while remaining readable.

## Principle statement
Large, short, declarative. Examples: **EVIDENCE FIRST.** and **DON'T FORCE THE SHAPE.** Do not stack multiple slogans.

## Evidence headline
Sentence case, aligned to the dominant field. It may intentionally begin `u` or `3u` away from the structural axis.

## Body
Normal reading typography. No gimmicks.

## Technical notation
Monospace for filenames, commands, TYPE names, measured values, and source/provenance labels.

## Marginal notation
Small mono labels in a void slot or outside the main text column. Only real information; never fake coordinates/equations.

## Alignment interruption
Use at most one strong alignment break per section: headline on structural field, notation on observation field, body on readable column.

## Scale behavior
Large = principle + descriptor + annotation.
Medium = principle + descriptor.
Compact = principle or concise label.

Use system-accessible sans/mono stacks. Originality comes from layout grammar, not proprietary font dependence.
