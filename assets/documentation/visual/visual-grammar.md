# OX-DX Visual Grammar

The visual grammar is the **Interrupted Evidence Field**. It is a graphic notation system, not a second ontology.

## LINE — persistence carrier
A line may begin from an anchor, terminate before connection, disappear for measured interruption, reappear at another density, or split into unequal rails. Continuity must have a reason.

## NODE — witness point
Default nodes are square/diamond or cut-square forms derived from the master mark, not circles. Filled = active witness; outline = available anchor; split = unresolved; ghosted = projected/derived. Color is never the only cue.

## FRAME — measured context
A frame does not automatically enclose. At least one side is open/interrupted. It may define context, measurement, scope, or attention without implying ownership.

## LAYER — displaced evidence plane
Repeated layers change at least one property: persistence, interruption, density, anchor position, or scale. Do not stack identical translucent cards.

## VOID — active absence
A measured empty lane or gap. It may show unknown, not observed, separation, scope break, or unresolved transition.

## BREAK — explicit discontinuity
Use measured widths, normally `1u`, `2u`, or `3u`. A tiny witness tick may mark an observed interruption.

## TRACE — secondary persistence
An offset echo of primary geometry. Offset by `μ` or `u`, lower visual weight, may erode with distance, never replaces identity geometry.

## SIGNAL — structured recurrence
Signal is cadence, not glow. Use deterministic segment lengths `u`, `2u`, `3u`.

## DENSITY — frequency, not blur
Density is segment/grain frequency. Do not express it only through opacity.

## FIELD — compositional evidence space
A field organizes anchors, rails, voids, trace tails, and observation offsets without requiring complete enclosure.

## EDGE — directional boundary
Direction comes from unequal continuation, asymmetric cut, density shift, or anchor position. Arrowheads are optional.

## ANCHOR — stable witness invariant
A 14 px diamond at standard editorial scale with an optional short witness tick / trace tail.

## Construction law
Every OX-DX composition should contain at least three of:
1. one stable anchor;
2. one explicit interruption;
3. one intentional void;
4. one persistence change.

The terms above are visual-design concepts only and must never become ontology TYPEs, KINDs, graph edges, phases, or engine states.
