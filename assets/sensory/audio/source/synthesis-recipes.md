# OX-DX Audio Synthesis Recipes — SPEC_ONLY

These recipes are production guidance, not generated audio.

## GRAIN

Source options:
- short wood/metal-like dry micro transient;
- synthesized filtered noise burst;
- tightly enveloped non-tonal impulse.

Envelope:
- very fast attack;
- short decay;
- no long reverb.

## BREATH / NOISE

- filtered broadband noise;
- low level;
- short envelope;
- avoid sweeping white-noise risers.

Use for trace/interference/reveal.

## RAIL

- one or two restrained low-mid resonant partials;
- short decay;
- no major/minor melodic cadence.

Use for connection/persistence.

## WITNESS

Layer:
- Grain;
- tiny Rail tail;
- optional soft Breath.

Keep dry and precise.

## SETTLE

Start:
- two slightly unstable/inharmonic partials or noise + resonance.

End:
- lower noise;
- less beating;
- stable short resonance.

This communicates stabilization, not truth.

## FRICTION

Use the same sonic material with:
- a missing transient segment;
- clipped/shortened decay;
- interrupted noise;
- lower resonance.

Do not introduce a separate “error siren” palette.

## Production test

Every family must:
- remain recognizable at low volume;
- work in mono;
- avoid painful high-frequency peaks;
- sound related to other OX-DX events;
- remain optional for comprehension.
